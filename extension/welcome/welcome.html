Welcome to Manga Mining

Starting…

OCR runs on your computer, so the text-recognition models (about 340 MB with WebGPU or about 190 MB on the CPU) download once. After that the extension starts in seconds. The extension never uploads page images. Explanations send the text of a bubble you click to the free server or to your own provider, unless you turn them Off in Settings.

While you wait

  1. Open a manga page.
  2. Click the extension icon in the toolbar to open the side panel.
  3. Click “Enable on this site”.
  4. Click a speech bubble to see its reading, a translation and why it's written that way.

You can close this tab: the download keeps going in the background.
