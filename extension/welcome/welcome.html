Welcome to Manga Mining

Starting…

OCR runs on your computer, so the text-recognition models (about 340 MB) download once. After that the extension starts in seconds. Page images never leave your computer.

While you wait

  1. Open a manga page.
  2. Click the extension icon in the toolbar to open the side panel.
  3. Click “Enable on this site”.
  4. Click a speech bubble to see its reading, a translation and why it's written that way.

You can close this tab: the download keeps going in the background.
